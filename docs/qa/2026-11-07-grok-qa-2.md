# First-time-user QA, round 2, 07 Nov 2026

> Source: Grok, a second first-time-user pass over the production console and the shared Arc Studio demos, 07 Nov 2026, about 02:27–02:39 IST. The full report is [2026-11-07-grok-qa-report-2.pdf](2026-11-07-grok-qa-report-2.pdf). Round 1 is [2026-11-07-grok-qa.md](2026-11-07-grok-qa.md).

Testnet only. This time the tester signed in with Privy and attempted one approved 0.01 USDC purchase. It was refused before any money moved, and the wallet still held 1.0 USDC afterwards.

## Verdict

**No: the console is not ready for a stranger's first paid attempt.** Sign-in and browsing work. The one purchase was signed once and refused for a deadline mismatch, before any funding.

## What worked this time

- **Sign-in.** Privy email sign-in succeeded on production. The embedded wallet connected on Arc testnet, chain 5042002.
- **Funds stayed put.** After the refused attempt, an independent read of the chain showed the wallet's USDC unchanged. No funding log exists for the job, and the authorization nonce was unused.
- **Verify** still read an earlier slip as `VALID`, and the same slip with its amount doubled as `INVALID`.
- **Hold.** The press-and-hold control works on a true press of two seconds or more.

**The cause of C1**, found in the code after the report:
- **The check.** The hub refuses on the countersigned job `deadline`, not on the authorization's `validBefore`. At verify, the escrow rail (`packages/rail/src/escrow/index.ts`) requires the signed deadline to be no earlier than now plus the offer's deadline, less a 15-second skew.
- **The console's side.** The console sets that deadline when it prepares the terms (`apps/web/src/lib/purchase.ts`).
- **The effect.** So any pause of more than about 15 seconds between preparing and the hub's check is refused, not only the eight-minute pause in this test.

## Withdrawn or not confirmed

- **"Signed by your wallet 0xf4d0…971b"** is a misleading label, not evidence that another key signed. The connected wallet stayed `0xe7ad…2AE3`.
- **The refused purchase owes no refund.** No funding transaction exists.
- **A changing Studio offer hash** is not evidence of a fake published listing.
- **Not tested:** an uninterrupted fast checkout. The report does not claim it always fails the same way.

## Missing in the first two minutes (section E)

2. Who can trigger a refund, on which rail, and when.
3. One map of live, listed and declared rails, and which escrow address is current.
4. A preflight check, before Sign opens, that the authorization's `validBefore` still covers the offer's deadline.
5. Seller display names, or a stated anonymity policy.
6. A demo label on the Arc Studio share and the Vercel app.
